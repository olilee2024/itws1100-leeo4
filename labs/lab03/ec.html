UX DESIGN: WIREFRAMES

  • Amazon Wireframe

    Highschool Class: Introduction to UX Design

    In my Introduction to UX Design, students had to redesign the UX design of any

    company. As a result, I decided to redesign Amazon. In the drawing above, my teacher

    assigned us to copy the current UX design the company has in order to understand how

    to curate an organized wireframe. In Amazon's original design there was a lot

    of clutter, leading users to be confused on how to access certain functions.

  • Roomie Wireframe

    Highschool Class: IB Business Management SL

    In my IB Business Management SL, my group and I had to curate

    a business proposal on a product that we believed would be successful

    towards a younger audience. As prospective college students, we decided

    to create an app proposal that would help incoming freshman find roommates

    and connect with new students! In the drawing above, I created a wireframe

    that would be an example of how the app would look: displaying the

    home page, the profile viewing page,and the chat page.

TECHNOLOGY: KODE WITH KLOSSY

In summer of 2023, I attended a mobile app development camp called Kode with Klossy. At this camp, I was taught how to use XCode to create and finalize our own project.

  • This was one of the first projects on XCode

    where students had to make text appear after clicking

    on the "Get to know me!" button.

  • This was the next project where students had

    to make a successful quiz model. Here I added

    that when you touch an "answer" button, an image

    appears after clicking on it.

  • As the last project, we had to code on XCode our group app

    proposal. For my group, we created an app called GlowHigh!

    GlowHigh is an app meant for people that struggle with time management.

    The wireframe above represents the services we provide on the app:

    calendar page, tasks page, habit tracker page, and journal entry page.
