Lab 07: Project Mockups

In lab 07, I created a project mockup for my Intro to Information Technology and Web Science class with my group. Our project is called ScheduleSync which takes the courses students are taking during a given semester and compares them to find the lectures, recitations, labs, and exam blocks that other students have together. Additionally, ScheduleSync can also find times where none of the students have classes so that they can get together and hang out.
